Bound Weapon Master

Prerequisite: the Spellcasting or Pact Magic class feature.

You are an expert in creating weapons out of pure magic, gaining the following benefits:

  • You learn one of the bound weapon spells below, provided you have a spell slot large enough to cast it. You can cast the spell using any spell slots you have.
  • You have advantage on checks to maintain concentration on a bound weapon spell.
  • Each bound weapon gains an additional benefit, depending on the spell:
    • Bound Dagger. The dagger(s) thrown range increases to 60/180.
    • Bound Sword. When you manifest the sword, you may also create a spectral shield in your other hand, provided its free. You are considered to be proficient with the shield and it provides a +2 bonus to AC. While the spell persists you can use a bonus action to cause the shield to disappear or reappear. Dropping or doffing the shield causes it to disappear at the end of turn.
    • Bound Axe. When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with the axe, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.
    • Bound Bow. Attacks with the bow ignores half and three-quarters cover.
