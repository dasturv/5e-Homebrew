Monk: Way of the Dancer

Dancers turn the flowing movements of dance into a graceful martial art, weaving between friends and foes with effortless movements. Rather than fight alone, they draw their companions into their rhythm, leading them across the battlefield to evade blows and strike in perfect unison.

Bonus Proficiency

When you choose this tradition at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the Performance skill. If you are already proficient in Performance, you instead gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.

Lead the Dance

Also at 3rd level, you weave your attacks into a flowing dance that creates openings for your companions. Whenever you make a melee attack against a creature, that creature can't make opportunity attacks against you or your allies for the rest of the turn.

Additionally, when you use Step of the Wind, once before the end of the turn when you move within 5 feet of a willing creature, you can allow that creature to use its reaction to move up to half its speed. This movement provokes opportunity attacks as normal.

Perfect Poise

At 6th level, your dance can help protect your partner. When you use Patient Defense, you can choose one willing creature within 5 feet of you. That creature can use its reaction to take the Dodge action.

Battle Dancer

Starting at 11th level, you can lead your partner into a destructive dance. When you use Flurry of Blows, you can choose one willing creature within 5 feet of you. That creature can use its reaction to make one weapon attack.

Grand Revelry

By 17th level, your dancing a merriment are downright infectious. Your Lead the Dance, Perfect Poise, and Battle Dancer features can affect up to two willing creatures instead of one. Additionally, whenever one of those features requires a willing creature to be within 5 feet of you, that range increases to 10 feet.
