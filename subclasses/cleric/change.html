Cleric: Change Domain

Clerics of the Change Domain are bringers of upheaval, whether that be destruction, revolution or just chaos pure and simple. They seek to undo all that stagnates and make certain everything keeps moving for good or ill.

Change Domain Spells
Cleric Level Spells
1st Chaos Bolt, Grease
3rd Knock, Shatter
5th Blink, Dispel Magic
7th Confusion, Freedom of Movement
9th Animate Objects, Transmute Rock

Bonus Proficiencies

When you choose this domain at 1st level, you gain proficiency with martial weapons.

Catalyst

At 1st level, your god's presence manifests in unpredictable ways. Whenever you cast a cleric spell of 1st level or higher, you can roll a d6 on the Catalyst table and immediately apply the result. Unless otherwise stated, you choose any creatures, spaces, or other effects created by the result.

Catalyst Effects
d6 Effect
1 Displacement. Choose one creature affected by the spell. You teleport it up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see that can safely support it.
2 Turn the Blow. Choose one creature affected by the spell. The next time that creature takes damage before the end of your next turn, you can increase or reduce the damage by 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier.
3 Warped Angles. Choose one creature affected by the spell. Until the end of your next turn, it either gains the benefits of half cover, or gains no benefit from half cover or three-quarters cover.
4 Shifting Ground. Choose a 10-foot-square area of ground you can see within 30 feet of you. Until the end of your next turn, normal terrain in that area becomes difficult terrain, or difficult terrain in that area becomes normal terrain.
5 Veil of Color. Choose a 10-foot cube you can see within 30 feet of you. The area becomes heavily obscured until the end of your next turn.
6 Chain Reaction. Roll twice more on this table, rerolling any additional 6s, and apply both results.

Ever-Changing

Also at 1st level you gain proficiency with one skill of your choice. Whenever you finish a long rest, you can replace this proficiency with proficiency in another skill of your choice.

Channel Divinity: Upheaval

Beginning at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to bring about change through sudden destruction. As an action, choose a point on the ground you can see within 60 feet of you. The ground in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on that point violently buckles and shifts. Each creature on the ground in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw against your cleric spell save DC. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 + your cleric level bludgeoning damage and falls prone. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and doesn't fall prone.

The ground in the area becomes difficult terrain until it is cleared or repaired. Objects and structures in the area that aren't being worn or carried also take the damage.

Sudden Escalation

Starting at 6th level, you can cause the world around you to become dangerously unstable, accelerating conflict toward its conclusion. As a bonus action, choose a point you can see within 60 feet of you. Until the start of your next turn, a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point is filled with volatile magic. Creatures within the area have advantage on attack rolls, and attack rolls against them have advantage.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Divine Strike

At 8th level, you gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with the power of change. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 force damage to the target. When you reach 14th level, the extra damage increases to 2d8.

Agent of Chaos

By 17th level, your presence causes violence to rapidly escalate as each decisive moment gives rise to another. Your melee weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. In addition, when a creature hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to make one melee weapon attack against that creature.

Additionally, when a creature within 10 feet of you scores a critical hit or reduces another creature to 0 hit points with a melee weapon attack, it can use its reaction to make one melee weapon attack against a creature of its choice within its reach.
