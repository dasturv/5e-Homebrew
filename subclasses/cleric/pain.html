Cleric: Pain Domain

Clerics of the Pain Domain see suffering as both sensation and power. Through intimate study of agony, they learn to endure wounds that would break another's focus and inflict torments upon their foes that blur the line between injury and imagination.

Pain Domain Spells
Cleric Level Spells
1st Bane, Inflict Wounds
3rd Blindness/Deafness, Hold Person
5th Bestow Curse, Vampiric Touch
7th Confusion, Phantasmal Killer
9th Hold Monster, Synaptic Static

Bonus Cantrip

At 1st level, you learn the Mind Sliver cantrip. It counts as a cleric cantrip for you and does not count against the number of cleric cantrips you know.

Student of Pain

Also at 1st level, you have an intimate relationship with suffering. You gain proficiency in Medicine.

Additionally, whenever you make a Constitution saving throw to maintain your concentration as a result of taking damage, you can add your Wisdom modifier to the saving throw.

Channel Divinity: Overwhelming Agony

Beginning at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to overwhelm a creature with debilitating pain. As an action, choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The creature must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your cleric spell save DC or fall prone and become incapacitated until the end of your next turn.

Phantom Pain

Starting at 6th level, you can inflict wounds that exist only for as long as your magic persists. When you cast a cleric spell that requires concentration and forces one or more creatures to make a saving throw, you can choose one creature that fails the saving throw. That creature takes 3d8 psychic damage. This damage can't reduce the creature below 1 hit point.

When the spell ends, if the creature is still alive, it regains hit points equal to the psychic damage it took from this feature.

The damage of this feature increases to 5d8 at 11th level and 7d8 at 17th level.

Potent Spellcasting

At 8th level, you add your Wisdom modifier to the damage you deal with any cleric cantrip.

Exquisite Suffering

By 17th level, you have mastered the art of drawing power from pain; both yours and that of others. A creature that has half its hit points or fewer has disadvantage on the first saving throw it makes against a cleric spell of 1st level or higher you cast.

Additionally, once per round when you succeed on a Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration as a result of taking damage, a creature affected by your Phantom Pain feature takes an additional 2d8 psychic damage. This damage is considered damage dealt by your Phantom Pain feature.
