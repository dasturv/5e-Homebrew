Wizard: Sonomancy

Sonomancers study the resonant threads that underlie magic and matter, learning to recognize the unique frequencies of those touched by their spells. By bringing these resonances into harmony or dissonance, they can reinforce allies, disrupt foes, and weave connections through which magic itself can reverberate.

Resonant Attunement

When you join this school at 2nd level, you gain the ability to recognize and attune yourself to the unique resonances revealed when your magic interacts with another creature.

After you cast a wizard spell of 1st level or higher, you can cause one creature affected by the spell to resonate with you for 1 minute. You cannot have more than one resonance on a creature, and you can maintain a number of resonances equal to your intelligence modifier. If you establish a new resonance beyond this limit, you choose which one to end.

While a creature resonates with you, you can expend its resonance in one of the following ways:

  • Harmony. When the creature fails a saving throw against a spell or other magical effect, you may use your reaction to allow it to reroll the saving throw. It must use the new roll.
  • Dissonance. When the creature succeeds on a saving throw against a wizard spell you cast, you can use your reaction to deal thunder damage to it equal to your Intelligence modifier + the level of the spell.

Sympathetic Resonance

By 6th level, your ability to attune yourself to the resonance of others has deepened. The duration of your Resonant Attunement increases to 1 hour.

Additionally, when you cast a wizard spell with a range of Touch, you can target a creature resonating with you within 30 feet as though it were within your reach.

Arcane Feedback

Starting at 10th level you have learned how to follow the vibrations of magic back to their source. When you are affected by a spell cast by a creature within 120 feet of you, you can cause the caster to resonate with you.

Additionally, when you successfully counter a spell, the caster takes thunder damage equal to your Intelligence modifier + the level of the countered spell. You can then cause the caster to resonate with you.

Resonance Cascade

By 14th level, You have learned to manipulate a complex web of resonances simultaneously, allowing changes in one to reverberate through the others. When you establish Resonance as part of casting a wizard spell of 1st level or higher, you can cause any number of creatures affected by the spell to resonate with you, rather than only one. You still can still only maintain a number of resonances equal to your intelligence modifier.

Additionally, when you expend a creature's Resonance, you can cause the effect to cascade through other resonating creatures:

  • Harmony. When you expend a creature's Resonance to use Harmony, each other resonating creature of your choice within 30 feet of it that is affected by the same spell or magical effect gains a 1d4 bonus to its saving throw against that effect. This bonus can turn a failed saving throw into a successful one.
  • Dissonance. When you expend a creature's Resonance to use Dissonance, each other resonating creature of your choice within 30 feet of it takes thunder damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
