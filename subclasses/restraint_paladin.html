Paladin: Oath of Restraint

Paladins who swear the Oath of Restraint harbor a darkness within them, whether born of a curse, unnatural bloodlust or simply a violent nature. Rather than deny these urges, however, these paladins temper them through discipline; learning when to hold them at bay and when to deliberately set them loose.

Tenets of Restraint

A paladin who swears this oath must follow these tenets:

Know your Hunger. Do not deny the darkness within you. To refuse to acknowledge what you are means you have no control over it.

Hold the Reins. Desire is not your master. No provocation, temptation nor passion excuses losing control.

Choose your Indulgence. Restraint is not abstinence. When your darker nature can serve a worthy purpose, it may be unleashed - but only deliberately.

Bear the Consequences. Your hunger is yours alone. Whatever you choose to do with it, take responsibility for the harm your choices cause.

Channel Divinity

When you take this oath at 3rd level, you gain the following two Channel Divinity options.

  • Feed the Hunger. As a bonus action, you can use your Channel Divinity to give in to your aggressive nature for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated. When you activate this feature, and as a bonus action on each of your turns for the duration, you can move up to your speed toward a hostile creature you can see. You must end this movement closer to that creature than you started. Additionally, whenever you reduce a creature to 0 hit points during the duration, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier + half your paladin level (rounded down).
  • Restrain the Hunger. As a reaction when a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your Channel Divinity to force the creature to reroll the attack roll. The creature must use the new roll. For 1 minute, or until you are incapacitated, whenever a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to force it to reroll the attack roll and use the new roll.

Aura of Suppression

Starting at 7th level, your presence tempers the aggressive instincts of your foes. Hostile creatures within 10 feet of you can't make opportunity attacks.

At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

Sate the Appetite

By 15th level, your mastery over your darker nature allows you to tap into it while in danger. Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon attack and have half your hit points or fewer, you can deal an additional 2d8 radiant damage with that attack and regain hit points equal to the extra damage dealt.

Apex Predator

At 20th level, you can fully unleash the hunger within while retaining complete mastery over it. As a bonus action, you can assume an unleashed form for 1 minute, or until incapacitated. While in this state you gain the following benefits:

  • Your movement speed increases by 20 feet.
  • You can use your Sate the Appetite feature regardless of your current hit points.
  • Making an opportunity attack does not expend your reaction.
  • You have advantage on attack rolls against creatures that have half their hit points or fewer.

Once you use this feature you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
