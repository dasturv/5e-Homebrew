Fighter: Ardent

An Ardent is a warrior who looks inwards, tapping their inner self to draw upon powers others could only dream of. Ardents use techniques similar to Mystics of the Immortal and Avatar schools, drawing upon the innate strength within their bodies and souls. An Ardent can be an inspiring leader, menacing foe or unshakable warrior.

Psionics

When you reach 3rd level, you augment your martial prowess with the ability to use psionics. Psionics is a special form of magic use, distinct from spellcasting, made up of practices instead of spells. A psionic practice cannot be affected by abilities that target spells, such as Counterspell, but are still affected by abilities that target magic in general, such as Dispel Magic.

Psionic Talents

A psionic talent is a minor psionic practice you have mastered. At 1st level, you know two psionic talents of your choice. You learn an additional talent of your choice at 15th level. You chose your talents from the Psionic Talents list.

Psionic Disciplines

A psionic discipline is a rigid set of mental exercises that allows a practitioner to manifest psionic power. An Ardent masters only a few disciplines at a time, and choses them from the Psionic Discipline list.

At 3rd level, you know two psionic disciplines of your choice that are from the Avatar or Immortal mystic orders. You learn an additional discipline at 10th level.

Psi Points

You have an internal reservoir of energy that can be devoted to psionic disciplines you know. This energy is represented by your Psi points. Each psionic discipline describes a list of practices you can create with it by spending psi points. A psionic talent requires no psi points.

The number of psi points you have is based on your fighter level, as shown in the Psi Points column of the Ardent Psionics table. Whenever you complete a long rest, you recover all expended psi points.

Psi Limit

Though you have access to a potent amount of psionic energy, it takes training and practice to channel that energy. There is a limit on the number of psi points you can spend on psionic practices each turn. The limit is based on your fighter level, as shown in the Psi Limit column of the Ardent Psionics table. For example, as a 7th-level fighter, you can spend no more than 3 psi points on practices each turn, no matter how many psi points you have.

Psionic Restrictions

You cannot use psionic talents nor spend psi points on psionic practices while wearing armor or using a shield that you are not proficient with.

Psionic Ability

Intelligence is your psionic ability for your psionic practices. You use your Intelligence modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a psionic practice or when making an attack roll with one.

Psionic save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier

Psionic attack modifier = your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier

Psychic Warrior

By 7th level you have improved your capabilities of weaving your martial and psionic abilities together. Whenever you use an action to use a psionic talent on your turn, you can make a weapon attack as a bonus action.

Ardent Recovery

Starting at 10th level, when you use your Second Wind ability, you may recover a number of spent psi points equal to your proficiency bonus. Once you do so, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest.

Improved Psychic Warrior

Starting at 18th level, when you use your action to use a psionic practice, you can make a weapon attack as a bonus action.
