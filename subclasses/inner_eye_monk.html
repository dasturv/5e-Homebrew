Monk: Way of the Inner Eye

Monks following the path of the Inner Eye look deeper into themselves than almost any other. Harnessing the powers of mind, body and spirit, they manifest great powers through both discipline and will. While many see the powers these monks wield simply as another form of psionics, the monks of this path would argue that they practice something older and more intuitive than that wielded by mystics.

Disciple of the Inner World

When you choose this tradition at 3rd level, you learn psionic techniques that harness the power of body, mind and soul. A technique requires you to spend ki points each time you use it.

You know two psionic techniques of your choice. You learn one additional psionic technique of your choice at 6th, 8th, 11th, 14th and 17th level.

Whenever you learn a new psionic technique, you can also replace one psionic technique that you already know with a different technique.

Using Psionic Practices. Some psionic techniques allow you to use practices. To use one of these practices, you use its action costs and other rules, and spend ki points instead of psi points.

The maximum number of ki points you can spend to use a practice in this way is determined by your monk level, as shown in the Practices and Ki Points table.
