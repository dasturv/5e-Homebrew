Warlock: Great Wyrm

Within the world there are dragons, mighty and powerful beings who wield immeasurable arcane might. And then there are the Great Wyrms. These ancient beings have neared divinity with their power, and have the ability to share a tiny fraction of it with their followers. The Great Wyrms vary wildly with their motivations and aims, but many find having mortal servants to deal with threats not worth their individual time most useful.

Dragon's Breath

Also at 1st level, you gain the ability to channel a fraction of your patron's elemental breath. As an action you can exhale elemental power in a 15-foot cone or 30-foot long, 5-foot wide line, depending on your patron's breath weapon. Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure a creature takes 2d6 damage of the type associated with your patron, or half as much on a success.

You have one use of this feature, and recover all uses when you complete a short or long rest.

The damage of this feature increases by 2d6 at certain levels: 6th (4d6), 10th (6d6) and 14th (8d6).

Wyrmscales

At 6th level your powers increase, granting you a portion of your patron's resilience. As a reaction when you are hit by an attack, you may gain a bonus to your AC equal to your Charisma modifier, potentially turning that hit into a miss.

In addition, your Dragon's Breath feature improves. You can use it twice between long rests and its area increases to a 30-foot cone or 60-foot long, 5-foot wide line, depending on your patron's type.

Gift of the Dragon

Beginning at 10th level, your patron's influence becomes even stronger. You gain resistance to the damage type associated with your patron.

Additionally, you gain one of the following benefits, depending on your patron's family:

  • Chromatic. When you roll damage for your Dragon's Breath ability or a warlock spell of 1st level or higher, you may reroll a number of those damage dice up to your Charisma modifier. You must keep the new rolls.
  • Gem. When you use your Dragon's Breath feature or cast a warlock spell of 1st level or higher, you may teleport up to 20 feet.
  • Metallic. When you use your Dragon's Breath feature or cast a warlock spell of 1st level or higher that forces other creatures to make a saving throw, you can choose a number of those creatures up to your Charisma modifier. A chosen creature automatically succeeds on its saving throw, and takes no damage if the effect deals half damage on a successful save.

Draconic Transformation

Starting at 14th level, you can channel the awesome presence of your patron. As a bonus action you can transform into a draconic form for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated. While transformed you gain the following benefits:

  • You have a fly speed equal to your walk speed and can hover. Spectral draconic wings sprout from your back.
  • You have immunity to the damage type associated with your patron. Magical scales appear over your body.
  • When you first transform, you recover all spent uses of your Dragon's Breath feature. Elemental power such as sparks, snow or embers whip around you.
  • When you first transform you let out a deafening roar. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you that can see or hear you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or be frightened of you for the duration of the transformation. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect for itself on a success.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
