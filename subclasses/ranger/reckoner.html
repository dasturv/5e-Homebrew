Ranger: Reckoner

Reckoners are rangers who dedicate themselves to balancing debts and settling grievances. Whether acting as mediators, trackers, or enforcers, they relentlessly pursue those who wrong others, protecting the victims and ensuring no debt goes unanswered.

Reckoner Magic

Also at 3rd level, you learn an additional spell when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown in the Reckoner Spells table. Each spell counts as a ranger spell for you, but it doesn't count against the number of ranger spells you know.

Call to Reckoning

When you choose this conclave at 3rd level, you hold others accountable for the harm they cause. When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you deals damage to you or another creature, you can use your reaction to cause the attacker to owe a Debt to the damaged creature. A Debt lasts for 1 hour or until you become incapacitated.

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature that owes a Debt with a weapon attack, you can deal an extra 1d8 damage to it. When you deal this extra damage, you can choose to settle the Debt. If you do, the Debt ends and the creature to whom the Debt was owed gains temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1 temporary hit point).

A creature can owe only one Debt at a time. If you cause it to owe a new Debt, its previous Debt ends.

Keeper of Accords

Also at 3rd level, you become adept at finding those with unsettled accounts. You gain proficiency in your choice of the Insight or Investigation skill. Additionally, you have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to track a creature that currently owes a Debt through your Call to Reckoning feature.

Intercede

Starting at 7th level, you can intervene when a debtor attempts to cause further harm. When a creature within 30 feet of you that owes a Debt makes an attack roll against the creature to whom it owes that Debt, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll.

Whether the attack hits or misses, you can move up to half your speed toward the attacker. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

Due Recompense

At 11th level, you become increasingly adept at bringing unsettled debts to a close. You gain the following bonuses:

  • The extra damage dealt by your Call to Reckoning feature increases to 2d8
  • When you settle a Debt using your Call to Reckoning feature, you gain the same number of temporary hit points as the creature to whom the Debt was owed.
  • Debts from your Call to Reckoning feature now last 8 hours.

Bear the Burden

By 15th level, you can bear the burdens of those under your protection. When you use your Intercede feature and the attack still hits, you can choose to take the damage from the attack in place of the creature to whom the Debt is owed. The damage you take can't be reduced or prevented in any way. You can then move up to half your speed toward the attacker without provoking opportunity attacks, as normal for your Intercede feature.
