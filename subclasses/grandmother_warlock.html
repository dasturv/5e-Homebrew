Warlock: Grandmother

Deep in the foulest swamps, most remote mountains, lifeless oceanic trenches and darkest corners of the lower planes lie the greatest of the hags: the Grandmothers. Profane matriarchs of secretive covens, these mighty fey practice their dark and strange arts far from the sight of others. Yet even they have need of the outside world. Whether to bring plots to life, grow their coven or simply to entertain them, Grandmothers make ample use of mortal servants, trading their esoteric power for obedience.

Expanded Spell List

At 1st level, the Grandmother lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell. The Grandmother Expanded Spells table shows the hag spells that are added to the warlock spell list for you.

Grandmother Expanded Spells
Spell Level Hag Spells
1st Absorb Elements
2nd Dragon's Breath
3rd Protection from Energy
4th Polymorph
5th Summon Draconic Spirit

Dragon's Wrath

Also at 1st level, your patron's elemental powers infuse your being, empowering your spells. Once on each of your turns when you deal damage with a warlock spell, you can deal extra damage to one of the targets equal to your proficiency bonus. The type of this damage is determined by the breath weapon of your patron.

Draconic Gift

At 6th level your elemental powers increase, granting you resistance to the damage type associated with your patron.

In addition, you can give yourself a flying speed equal to your move speed that lasts for 10 minutes, during which you can hover. Once you use this feature you cannot use it again until you complete a short rest.
