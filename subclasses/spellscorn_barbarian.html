Barbarian: Spellscorn

Barbarians who follow the Path of the Spellscorn possess a deep and instinctive defiance toward magic. Their rage allows them to shrug off supernatural influence, tear apart spells, and violently retaliate against those who attempt to hinder them.

Spiteful Retort

When you start down this path at 3rd level, you learn to channel your rage to resist and punish the powers of magic. While raging you have advantage on saving throws against spells.

Additionally, when you succeed on a saving throw from a hostile creature while raging, you may use your reaction to move up to half your movement speed and make a melee attack against a creature within your reach.

Scornful Defiance

By 6th level, your ability to attune yourself to the resonance of others has deepened. The duration of your Resonant Attunement increases to 1 hour.

Additionally, when you cast a wizard spell with a range of Touch, you can target a creature resonating with you within 30 feet as though it were within your reach.

Rend Magic

Starting at 10th level, you can violently tear apart magic affecting your foes. Once during each of your rages, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can choose one spell affecting that creature. The caster of that spell must make a Constitution saving throw against a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier. The caster can choose to fail this saving throw.

Upon a failed save, the spell ends, and the creature you hit takes an additional 1d6 force damage for each level of the spell.

Unbridled Spite

By 14th level, your fury cannot be quelled by even the most debilitating effects. You can use your Spiteful Retort when you fail a saving throw as well as when you succeed. Additionally, when you use Spiteful Retort after succeeding on the saving throw, the attack deals an additional 2d6 damage on a hit.

Furthermore, when the attack granted by your Spiteful Retort hits a creature that is concentrating on a spell, the DC of its Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration can't be lower than 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier.
