Scraedai (Cicadas)
Hook

Formed of Song and Sound

It is believed by the Scraedai that they were not formed from lesser bugs like most races, as there is no wild bug resembling them; instead, they believe their beloved goddess Caelimynn sung them into being, pulling upon the resonant threads of magic itself to create something wholly unique (an idea supported by the Scraedai's natural inclination towards sonomancy). It has been centuries since their goddess was wounded in battle against the Maidoir goddess Gulnach, and though she rests in restorative slumber deep beneath their home within the Whistlewood, the Scraedai still hold tightly to their creator.

A Culture of the Arcane

The Scraedai hold knowledge and mastery of the arcane over almost anything else. Every level of their loose society is built around magic, with almost every menial task taken care of through magical means. This lack of scarcity allows almost every Scraedai study in the ways of wizardry in some way, with even their soldiers following the ways of the eldritch knight.

"Good" Necromancy

While necromancy in Llorelan is usually tied with the loss of identity, corruption or the vile sacrifices of lichdom, the Scraedai, with their love of all arcane arts, have found an alternate path. The Scraedai regularly create undead out of their shed shells, called hollows, as servants. In fact, the practice is so common that giving someone a hollow is seen as a friendly gesture. Beyond this, the most respected members of Scraedai society are actually a form of lich. Known as Grandmasters, they are individuals who have mastered the arcane so thoroughly that they are able to bind their souls to their bodies by creating a "song" that perfectly captures every aspect of their identity. Most of the time these Grandmasters lay dormant, sitting dead upon an armchair or in front of a fireplace their servants keep lit. However, acolytes of the masters learn the unique song to the one they serve, and can sing portions of it to stir the ancient wizards. The act of stirring a Grandmaster does drain at one's soul, shortening one's own life, though the knowledge that can be gained from such a figure is often seen as beyond worthwhile to the acolytes. Usually, only a tiny fraction of the master's song is sung, and they stir only slightly. In this state they are often rambling and unfocused, with acolytes taking notes for any fragment of wisdom. To truly awaken a Grandmaster requires a choir of powerful wizards to work in unison to sing the song, an act that is likely to kill all involved. However, a fully awakened Grandmaster is a mighty force indeed, able to sweep aside entire armies on their own.

Distantly Jovial

The Scraedai tend to be logical and unbiased in their approach to life. However, despite their apparent coldness, most find the Scraedai quite agreeable. They are usually curious, quick to laughter and eager to discuss with others, though their casual acceptance of necromancy and dismissal of strict morality does put some off.

Scholars and Sages

While most Scraedai are content to live and die within the confines of their libraries and labratories, there are plenty who seek knowledge from the wider world. Whether out of curiosity or to test some esoteric theories, Scraedai are not uncommon to see far from their homelands.

Scraedai Traits

Regardless of what form they take, all Scraedai share the following traits.

  • Ability Score Increase. When determining your character's ability scores, increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different scores by 1. You can't raise any of your scores above 20.
  • Creature Type. You are an insectoid.
  • Size. Your size is medium. Scraedai tend to be squat and broad, ranging between 4 and 5 sprigs tall, and weigh up to 250 pebblets.
  • Speed. Your walking speed is 30 sprigs.
  • Darkvision. You can see in dim light within 60 sprigs of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light. You discern colors in that darkness only as shades of gray.
  • Fey Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the charmed condition on yourself.
  • Sonic Resistance. You have resistance to thunder damage.
  • Sonomancy Training. You know the Alter Sound cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level you can cast Thunderwave. Once you reach 5th level, you can also cast Shatter. Once you cast either spell with this trait, you cannot cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest; however, you can cast the spell using any Spell Slots you have of the appropriate level.
    • Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait. You choose which ability when you choose this lineage.
  • Wings. Starting at 5th level, you can use a bonus action to magically strengthen your wings for 1 minute. For the duration, you gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed and can hover. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until yout complete a long rest.
  • Languages. Your character can speak, read and write Click-clack and Scraedai. Scraedai is a language of long, droning syllables, with context and nuance given though the use of sonomantic magic.
