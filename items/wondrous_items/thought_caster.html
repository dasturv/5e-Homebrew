Thought Caster

Wondrous Item, uncommon

This crown of shell and psycho-amplifying materials is robustly made, with nobs and dials to attune it better to the wearer. When worn by a telepathic creature, it allows communication over great distances. While a creature with the ability to communicate telepathically wears the Thought Caster, they can focus on another telepathic creature they are familiar with. If the target is within 1 mile (or 8 miles if the target is also wearing a Thought Caster), they become aware of the user and can begin communicating back and forth telepathically. While communicating, both creatures must maintain concentration as if they are concentrating on a spell.

A non-telepathic creature gains no benefits for wearing a Thought Caster, and cannot be targeted by a Thought Caster.
