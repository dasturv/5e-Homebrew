A World of Bugs

Llorelan is a world that works on a fundamentally different scale from most. With all the intelligent races being derived from bugs and other tiny creatures, things play out quite differently. Trees are massive, able to house multiple cities upon their branches; stone is impossible to mine, with amber and shells replacing metals; water turns from an occasional nuisance to a deadly snare.

Religions

Llorelan has a rich diversity of gods and godlike entities, all worshipped in differing ways. Below is a list of several of these gods, alongside some of their most common followers.

Alowe

Alignment: LN
Domain(s): Light, Order

The sister to Evenala and creator of the Formids, Alowe and her beloved daughters stand unbroken in a chaotic world. The Formids are her zealous daughters and followers, with whom she lays claim to the depths of the earth. Alowe instills in her followers a sense of duty, teaching them to forget oneself in absolute commitment to their colony. She is, and by extention her followers, suspicious of anyone outside of the colonies. Alowe is seen as a source of light in dark places, an iron-willed ruler and bringer of law.

Alowe is worshipped most often alongside her sister, with Formids and Abellids being the bulk of her followers. That being said, a prayer to Alowe is commonplace in the Westlands for anyone going underground.

C'Tar

Alignment: N
Domain(s): Grave, Tempest

The long dead god of the Eqrab, C'Tar was slain in battle with the Great Wyrm. In life he was a mighty ruler of his people and consort to Zu'nuti, the spirit of the Savage Lands themselves. In death his mind and will is gone, but his rage still manifests in terrible sandstorms. Followers of C'Tar believe that the souls of the Eqrab go to him after death, joining him in the endless winds of the desert.

C'Tar is almost exclusively worshipped by the Eqrab, with most other races believing his death ended his influence.

Evenala

Alignment: LG
Domain(s): Forge, Peace

Creator of the Abellids and sister to the goddess Alowe, Evenala is the guiding force behind the greatest empire in Llorelan. She teaches her followers to live selflessly in service to their sisters and queens, and promotes a busy life full of crafts and community. She is seen as a caring matriarch, wise queen and fierce avenger for her fallen daughters.

Evenala is worshipped alongside her sister by the Abellids. Beside her daughters though, Elytrans and Formids pursuing art or diplomacy often hold her in high regard.

The Gashalag

Alignment: N
Domain(s): Nature

The Gashalag is an enigmatic force. Not fully conscious, yet according to its devotees, active in the world of Llorelan. The Gashalag is the embodient of the earth and nature, a primordial force that is said to predate even the gods. Those who follow the Gashalag aren't students nor followers in the traditional sense, instead seeking to live as a part of nature and bring balance where others disrupt it.

The Gashalag is most often worshipped by druids, with its worship being rare outside of esoteric groups.

The Great Wyrm

Alignment: LN
Domain(s): Order, Strength

The Great Wyrm was the most powerful god of the previous era. Before he succumbed to the venom of the Eqrab god C'Tar, the Great Wyrm dominated Llorelan. His divine presence was so great that he shaped the minds of mortals in Llorelan, with many great battles, legendary heroes and bitter conflicts defining the era. It was from his blood that the Harish were born, and it is his tenets of strength and discipline that still guide them to this day.

Most followers of the Great Wyrm are Harish, though there are others who believe such a mighty being can never truly die.

Khoradin

Alignment: LE
Domain(s): Order, War

Khoradin is the goddess of domination, pride and victory. Khoradin is one of the Dark Gods, alongside Syn and Vhorash. Khoradin and her brood form legions of fiends, creating bastions of iron-fisted rule within Gehenna where they strike out against all who would oppose them. Khoradin herself is a tyrant and warrior of unparalled skill, teaching her followers to grow strong and enforce their will upon the world.

Khoradin, much like the other Dark Gods, is rarely worshipped openly. Despite this, her association with strength and victory makes her worship all too common within militaries across Llorelan.

Noxella

Alignment: NG
Domain(s): Life, Twilight

Noxella is the goddess of the moon, grief and love. A gentle a caring deity, she is one of the Radiant Trio alongside Solassir and Stellis. Noxella takes the form of a silvery moth or Caelin, and resides within her realm in Celestia. She is a gentle and loving deity, always seeking to bring the meek and suffering back into her radiant light. Noxella teaches her followers to help those around them and heal what the world would break.

Noxella is worshipped across Llorelan, with her finding easy converts amongst the downtrodden and those who seek to help them.

Solassir

Alignment: LG
Domain(s): Light, War

Solassir is the goddess of the sun, honor and vengeance. She is the blinding light that strikes down the darkness, the holy fire that cleanses the world. Solassir is part of the Radiant Trio alongside Noxella and Stellis. She most often takes the form of a giant Caelin robed in scintillating colors and wielding a sword of fire. She is fierce and strict, teaching her followers to always be vigilant for evil and to root it out wherever it would hide.

Solassir is worshipped across Llorelan, with soldiers and paladins especially flocking to her worship.

Stellis

Alignment: CG
Domain(s): Trickery, Twilight

Stellis is the god of the stars, travellers and stories. He, unlike his siblings in the Radiant Trio, regularly wanders Llorelan itself. He takes the guise of an ordinary bug, joining travellers around their fires and sharing stories. Stellis loves good company and is quick with a joke, with gracious hosts recieving gifts and blessings. Those who would turn him away or threaten him, however, can often find themselves cursed with misfortune or madness. Through his escapades he helps to ensure the safety of travellers, as it is impossible to know if the stranger who wandered into camp is Stellis in disguise.

Stellis is invoked by anyone on the road, with his worship being particularly common amongst the Amhrani.

Syn

Alignment: NE
Domain(s): Knowledge, Pain

Syn is the god of secrets, pain and pleasure. He is one of the Dark Gods, made up of himself, Khoradin and Vhorash. Syn lies hidden deep within his domain in Gehenna, in a place no one, not even the other Dark Gods, can find. From his realm he pulls upon mortals with threads of lies and promises. It is said no one has ever seen the true form of Syn, as he always takes the form most alluring to his followers and victims alike. Syn teaches his followers to be clever, enjoy the pleasures of life and endure the suffering with as much zeal.

Syn is very rarely openly worshipped, with only the Doraithe Amhrani having any widespread acceptance of him. That being said, he is still prayed to by those needing to endure great hardship or those wishing to not be discovered.

Vhorash

Alignment: CE
Domain(s): Change, Death

Vhorash is the goddess of murder, destruction and change. She rampages across Gehenna as part beast, part natural disaster. She holds no care for her brood, who flee from their mother as soon as they are born, wreaking havoc wherever they go. Despite her beastial and destructive nature, Vhorash is not mindless. She is a cunning and spiteful being, one who seeks to bring all low and end anything that seeks to persist. She teaches her few followers that all things are temporary, and the only real truth is impermanence.

Vhorash is very rarely worshipped, as she cares nothing for mortals whether they pledge themselves to her or not. Though there are few who do call upon her, especially revolutionaries and those seeking to not just defeat but utterly destroy their foes.
