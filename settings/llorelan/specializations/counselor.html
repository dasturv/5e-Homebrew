Counselor

Prerequisite: proficiency in Persuasion.

You have recieved special training in maintaining the spiritual and mental health of your peers. You gain the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. Whenever another creature within 30 sprigs of you that can see and hear you would fail a saving throw, you may grant a bonus to their total equal to your proficiency bonus, potentially turning a failure to a success. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a short rest.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you learn the Guidance cantrip. If you already know Guidance, you instead learn one Cleric cantrip of your choice. Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip is Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (chosen when you gain this rank).
  • Rank 3. As part of a short rest, you can spend time attending to and inspiring another creature. At the end of the short rest that creature gains temporary hit points equal to your level.
