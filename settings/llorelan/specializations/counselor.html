Counselor

Prerequisite: proficiency in Insight.

You have recieved special training in maintaining the spiritual and mental health of your peers. You gain the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. Over a period of 10 minutes, you can guide and inspire another creature. At the end of that 10 minutes, that creature gains an Inspiration, and all other creatures that have an Inspiration from this feature loses that Inspiration. Once you use this feature you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you learn the Guidance cantrip. If you already know Guidance, you instead learn one Cleric cantrip of your choice. Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip is Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (chosen when you gain this rank).
  • Rank 3. Whenever a creature would use an Inspiration gained from this feature, they may roll three d20s instead of two and keep the higher result.
