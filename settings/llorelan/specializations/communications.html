Communications

Prerequisite: Intelligence 12 or greater.

You have recieved training in the proper usage of magical communication methods, gaining the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. You can speak telepathically to creatures within 60 sprigs of you. Your telepathic utterances are in a language you know, and the creature understands you only if it knows that language. Your communication doesn't give the creature the ability to respond to you telepathically. If you already have the ability to communicate telepathically, the range of your telepathy increases by 30 sprigs instead.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, the range of your telepathy is doubled.
  • Rank 3. You can cast the Sending spell with this trait without requiring any components. Once you cast Sending with this feature, you can’t cast the spell with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast Sending using any spell slots you have.

Additionally, the Order provides you with a Thought Caster.
