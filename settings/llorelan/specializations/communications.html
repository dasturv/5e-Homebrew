Communications

You have recieved training in the proper usage of magical communication methods, gaining the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. You can speak telepathically to any creature you can see within 60 sprigs of you. Your telepathic utterances are in a language you know, and the creature understands you only if it knows that language. Your communication doesn't give the creature the ability to respond to you telepathically. When communicating with another telepathic creature, the range is increased to 1 bough and does not require sight, provided you are familiar with the target.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, your telepathy no longer requires sight, provided you are aware of your target. Furthermore its range is increased to 120 sprigs for non-telepathic creatures and 8 boughs for telepathic ones.
  • Rank 3. You can cast Sending. Once you use this feature you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
