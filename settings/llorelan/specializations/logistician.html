Logistician

Prerequisite: must be an officer.

You have recieved training in planning and managing supplies. You gain the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. Once a month, you may make a requisition for additional equipment for an official mission. The equipment must be reasonable for the given mission, and cannot have a total Perl value exceeding 5 x your level. If an item would reasonably be able to be returned after the mission, it must be.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, your Strength is considered to be 3 higher for the purposes of calculating your carrying capacity.
