Engineer

Prerequisite: proficiency in Carpenter's, Mason's or Wood Carver's Tools.

You have received training in creating the temporary structures and defenses required for war. You gain the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. The time it takes for you to set up temporary structures and static defenses is halved. Some examples of these are tents, barricades, traps, rough terrain and trenches.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Strength, Constitution or Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you learn the Mending cantrip. If you already know Mending, you learn one Artificer cantrip of your choice instead. Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting attribute for this spell (chosen when you gain this feature).
  • Rank 3. While you are gaining the benefits of cover, you gain a further +1 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws.

Additionally, the Order provides you with a trunk containing: A shovel, a handaxe, a knife, nettle rope (50 sp), and string (10 sp).
