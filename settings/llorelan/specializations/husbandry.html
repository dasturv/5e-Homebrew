Husbandry

Prerequisite: proficiency in Animal Handling.

You have been trained in how to train and manage beasts in battle. You gain the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. You can spend one week of downtime forming a special bond with a friendly beast. While bonded, you have advantage on all Animal Handling checks directed at the bonded creature, the creature gains a number of hit dice equal to your level, and its maximum hit points are increased by five times your level. You can only have one bonded beast this way at a time, with the older bond ending when you form a new one.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Strength, Dexterity or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you can mount or dismount a creature using only 5 sprigs of movement instead of half.
  • Rank 3. Your bonded beast gains a bonus to its saving throws equal to your proficiency bonus.

Additionally, the Order provides you with a riding saddle, bit and tackle, and animal feed.
