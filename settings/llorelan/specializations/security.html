Security

Prerequisite: proficiency with Shields.

You have recieved training in protecting a ward. You gain the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. You learn the Interception Fighting Style from the fighter class. If you already have that style, you instead choose a different Fighting Style from the fighter class.
  • Rank 2. Increase your Strength, Constitution or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, when you roll Initiative, you may choose to switch Iniatives with another willing creature who can hear you.
  • Rank 3. When you would use your reaction to intercept an attack using your Fighting Style, you may roll the d10 twice and keep either result.
