Assault Specialist

You have recieved training in intense, sudden combat. You gain the following benefits:

  • Rank 1. When you roll initiative and are not surprised, you can immediately move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks (no action required).
  • Rank 2. Increase your Strength, Dexterity or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, your movement speed is increased by 5 sprigs.
  • Rank 3. You have advantage on initiative rolls.
