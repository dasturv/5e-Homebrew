Skyrim: Enchantments

Enchantments in Skyrim replace the usual magic items, providing a more flexible framework.

Soul Gems and Charges

Some enchantments have charges, which limits how often their bonuses can be used. Though unlike regular DnD, enchanted items do not regain charges over time, instead requiring soul gems to recharge. Soul gems are crystals that store the souls of slain creatures, and are used extensively in enchanting. Each type of soul gem has a range of CRs that can be captured within itself. You can use an action to destroy a filled soul gem and restore charges in one enchanted item you are holding, with the amount of charges depending on the soul gem used. You can fill a soul gem with a soul of a lower tier, but the amount of charges recovered is based on the stored soul, not the gem itself.

Black Soul Gems
Most soul gems are known as white soul gems, and can be filled with the souls of any non-humanoid creature. Humanoids, however, can only have their soul stored in a black soul gem, regardless of CR. Black soul gems are highly illegal and are seen as blatantly evil by most.

Active Enchantments

Active enchantments, unlike passive ones, have charges. Each item rarity has a maximum number of charges and each rarity of enchantment uses a different amount of charges. The strength of the enchantment is chosen whe nthe item is created, and cannot be changed. An item can have an active enchantment of its rarity or lower. Active enchantments can only be applied to weapons. When you deal damage to a creature using a weapon with an active enchantment, you may expend the charges listed in the description to apply the effect, provided you have enough charges.
