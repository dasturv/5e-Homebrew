Skyrim: Enchantments

Enchanted Items

Instead of the traditional magic items found in DnD, the world of Skyrim has enchanted items. These enchanted items provide powerful boons to those who wield them. Each enchanted item has a rarity, which decides how strong of enchantments can be on the item as well as how many charges the item has. Unlike magic items in regular DnD, enchanted items do not recover charges over time, instead requiring soul gems to recharge. All enchanted items are considered to require attunement.

Soul Gems

Soul gems are crystals that store the souls of slain creatures, and are used extensively in enchanting. Each type of soul gem has a range of CRs that can be captured within itself. You can use an action to destroy a filled soul gem and restore charges in one enchanted item you are holding, with the amount of charges depending on the power of the soul used. You can fill a soul gem with a soul of a lower tier, but the amount of charges recovered is based on the stored soul, not the gem itself.

Black Soul Gems
Most soul gems are known as white soul gems, and can be filled with the souls of any non-humanoid creature. Humanoids, however, can only have their soul stored in a black soul gem, regardless of CR. Black soul gems are highly illegal and are seen as blatantly evil by most.

Enchanting Items

Creating an enchanted item takes patience, skill and plenty of arcane materials. To get started you will need access to an arcane enchanter and an unenchanted item you wish to enchant, then choose your item rarity and enchantment(s) out of the options available to you.

Item Rarity
The rarity of item you are creating dictates how powerful the enchantments the item can have, as well as how difficult it will be to create. You can create an item of a specific rarity only if you meet its requirements and provide the necessary materials. When you complete the associated extended check for the item, it is complete. If a creature helping on the extended check does not meet the requirements for the item or does not the the formulae for the selected enchantment(s), they make the check at disadvantage.

Staffs
Staffs are another type of enchanted item that can be used to cast spells. Creating a staff uses the same rules as other enchanted items, except instead of choosing enchantments, you choose a spell to infuse into the staff. First choose a spell level. You can choose any level of spell of 1st level and higher that you can cast and does not exceed the max spell level of the staff. Next, choose a spell that meets the following criteria:

  • You know or have the spell prepared.
  • The spell can be cast at the chosen level.
  • The spell has no material components that are consumed as part of its casting.

If the chosen spell has material components with a gp cost, you must provide those components while creating the staff.

You can cast the spell infused in a staff by expending a number of charges decided by its spell level (see the spell charges table below), provided the staff has enough charges left. The casting requires no components and if it requires a saving throw, the DC is decided by the rarity of the staff.

Choosing Enchantments
When choosing enchantments you can select any enchantments whose formulae you know, can be applied to the item, and is of the correct rarity or lower (as seen in the table above). By default players do not know any formulae, but can learn new formulae by disenchanting items. Disenchanting an item requires completing an Arcana Extended check (DC 75, 10 minute interval), after which the player learns the formulae for any enchantments on the item, though the item is destroyed in the process.
