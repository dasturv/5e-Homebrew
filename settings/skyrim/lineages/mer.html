Mer

Mer, or Elves, are the "Elder Races" of Mundus, such as: the Altmer, Bosmer, Dunmer, Dwemer, and Falmer—all of which are descendants of the primordial Aldmer race. The Orsimer (or Orcs) are also commonly believed to share this ancestry. The term Mer is also commonly used for non-elven peoples, like Betmer which refers to Beastfolk, and Nemer which seemingly refers to anyone who is not an elf. The terms "elven" and "merish" are adjectives meaning "of or relating to the Mer". Elves are said to consider themselves the only "truly human race", as they are descended directly from the gods. Some elves regard Mannish and Beast races as nothing more than intelligent animals.

What sets the mer apart from the other races of Tamriel is their long lifespans; aside from the Orsimer, Aldmeri lifespans typically range from several centuries to as much as a thousand years, though few ever reach this upper limit due to external factors like disease or violence. Elven longevity has been described as a kind of “godhood” that was “granted by the gods”. Lifespans in the thousands are typically seen among powerful mages or nobles. It is said Elven nobles live “a lot longer” than common folk due to “selective breeding” and a “luxurious lifestyle” (among other reasons). Some of the most ancient and powerful Elven mages have extended their lives far beyond the natural span, in some cases enduring for millennia through the use of potent magical practices.

Mer Traits

Regardless of what form they take, all Lineage share the following traits.

  • Ability Score Increase. When determining your character's ability scores, increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different scores by 1. You can't raise any of your scores above 20.
  • Creature Type. You are a humanoid.
  • Size. Your size is medium or small, chosen when you select this lineage. Mer tend to be between 4 and 7 feet tall, and tend weight slightly less than men, weighing up to 250 lbs.
  • Speed. Your walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Aldmeri Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.
  • Darkvision. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
  • Keen Senses. You have proficiency in the Perception skill.
  • Subrace. You choose a subrace and gain its features. Choose either Altmer, Bosmer or Dunmer.
Altmer

The Altmer, also known as High Elves by the denizens of Tamriel, by the archaic term Salache, or as the self-titled "Cultured People", are a tall, golden-skinned elven race hailing from the Summerset Isles. They regard themselves as the direct descendants of the Aedra and as the purest, most unaltered heirs of the Aldmer, the ancient elven race who first arrived on Tamriel from the mythical lost continent of Aldmeris.

  • Cantrip. You know one cantrip of your choice from the Wizard spell list. The spellcasting ability for it is the same as your Altmer Magic trait.
  • Altmer Magic. You know the Prestidigitation cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level you can cast Sleep. Once you reach 5th level, you can also cast Bound Sword. Once you cast either spell with this trait, you cannot cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest; however, you can cast the spell using any Spell Slots you have of the appropriate level.
    • Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait. You choose which ability when you choose this lineage.
  • Studious. You can read, speak, and write one additional language of your choice.
  • Languages. Your character can speak, read and write Common and Altmeris.
Bosmer

The Bosmer (formerly the Boiche) are the Elven clan-folk of Valenwood, a forested province in southwestern Tamriel. In the Empire, they are often referred to as Wood Elves, but they typically call themselves Bosmer and the Tree-Sap people. Bosmer rejected the stiff, formal traditions of Aldmeri high culture, preferring a more romantic, simple existence in harmony with the land. They are relatively nimble and quick in body compared to their more "civilized" Altmeri cousins (who often look down upon the Bosmer as unruly and naive). Their agility makes them well-suited as scouts and thieves. However, they are also a quick-witted folk, and many pursue successful careers in scholarly pursuits or trading.

  • Fleet-footed. Your movement speed is increased to 35 feet.
  • Bosmer Magic. You know the Druidcraft cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level you can cast Animal Friendship. Once you reach 5th level, you can also cast Protection from Poison. Once you cast either spell with this trait, you cannot cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest; however, you can cast the spell using any Spell Slots you have of the appropriate level.
    • Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait. You choose which ability when you choose this lineage.
  • Bosmer Weapon Training. You have proficiency with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow.
  • Languages. Your character can speak, read and write Common and Bosmeris.
Dunmer

The Dunmer, also known as Dark Elves, Ash-Elves by the Daedra, "Pureblooded Folk" by themselves, or the archaic Moriche, are the grey-skinned, typically red-eyed elven peoples of Morrowind. In the Empire, "Dark Elf" is the common usage, but they and their Aldmeri brethren prefer the term "Dunmer". "Dark" is variously interpreted as meaning "dark-skinned", "gloomy", and "ill-favored by fate", with the Dunmer and their national identity embracing these various connotations with enthusiasm.

  • Ash-born. You have resistance to fire damage.
  • Dunmer Magic. You know the Fire Bolt cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level you can cast Protection from Evil and Good. Once you reach 5th level, you can also cast Invisibility. Once you cast either spell with this trait, you cannot cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest; however, you can cast the spell using any Spell Slots you have of the appropriate level.
    • Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait. You choose which ability when you choose this lineage.
  • Dunmer Weapon Training. You have proficiency with rapiers, shortswords, and hand crossbows.
  • Languages. Your character can speak, read and write Common and Dunmeris.
