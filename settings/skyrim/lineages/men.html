Man

Man, meaning human, is used to refer to any of the human races of Mundus, such as the Nords, Imperials, Bretons, Reachmen and Redguards. While the Bretons are the result of interbreeding between Aldmeri and Nedes, they are most commonly considered men, and not mer. Men are known to have inhabited various continents including Atmora, Tamriel, and Yokuda. Some consider the races of Men to be less distinct than those of Mer, with more arbitrary boundaries.

Human Traits

Regardless of what form they take, all humans share the following traits.

  • Ability Score Increase. When determining your character's ability scores, increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different scores by 1. You can't raise any of your scores above 20.
  • Creature Type. You are a humanoid.
  • Size. Your size is medium or small, chosen when you select this lineage. Humans can be anywhere between 3 and 7 feet tall, and weigh up to 350 lbs.
  • Speed. Your walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Adaptable. You gain one additional positive trait of your choice.
  • Subrace. You choose a subrace and gain its features. Choose either Breton, Imperial, Nord, Reachman or Redguard.
  • Languages. Your character can speak, read and write Common and one other language of your choice.
Breton

Bretons (from the Ehlnofex beratu meaning "half") are a hybrid race of both human and elven ancestry that primarily inhabit their ancestral homeland of High Rock, and the Systres Archipelago. Bretondom originated from the Nedes of High Rock and Aldmer interbreeding for centuries as far back as the Merethic Era. This mixed ancestry gives Bretons a unique perspective on Nirn's oldest feud: the conflict between men and mer. The Bretons' Nedic ancestors are referred to as the Druids of Galen (or proto-Bretons) by retroactive nomenclature, while Clan Direnni are primarily credited as being their Aldmer ancestors. Bretons are also distantly related to the Reachfolk.

  • Aldmeri Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.
  • Cantrip. You know one cantrip of your choice from the Wizard spell list. Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this cantrip. You choose which ability when you choose this lineage.
  • Dragonskin. Whenever a spell would force you to make a saving throw, you may roll a d4 and add it to the total.
Imperial

The Imperials, also known as Cyrodiils, Cyrodilics, and Imperial Cyrods, are the well-educated and well-spoken natives of the civilized and cosmopolitan province of Cyrodiil. Imperials are also known for the discipline and training of their citizen armies, and their respect for the rule of law. Imperials have proved to be shrewd diplomats and traders, and these traits, along with their remarkable skill and training as infantry, have enabled them to colonize various other nations and create the Empire. Their hegemony has waxed and waned throughout the eras, and most historians refer to three distinct Empires, the ends of which each mark a new epoch in Tamrielic history.

  • Voice of the Emperor. You have proficiency in Persuasion.
  • Imperial Armor Training. You gain proficiency in either light armor or shields, chosen when you select this subrace. Additionally, armor you are wearing is considered to be only half its weight when calculating encumbrance.
  • Cosmopolitan. You can speak, read and write one additional language of your choice.
Nord

Nords, also known as Northmen, Bron in the Language of Dragons, or the Children of the Sky, are a race of tall and fair-haired humans from Skyrim who are known for their incredible resistance to cold and magical frost. They are fierce, strong and enthusiastic warriors, and many become renowned warriors, soldiers and mercenaries all over Tamriel. Eager to augment their martial skills beyond the traditional methods of Skyrim, they excel in all manner of warfare, and are known as a militant people by their neighbors. Nords were known for a time for their nautical prowess when they migrated from Atmora, and seafaring merchants or smugglers are not unheard of.

  • Acclimated. You have resistance to cold damage.
  • Nordic Weapon Training. You have proficiency with battleaxes, handaxes, light hammers and warhammers.
  • Battle Cry. As a bonus action, you can let out a roaring battle cry. Creatures of your choice within 10 feet of you that can hear you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you until the end of your next turn. The DC of the save equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
Reachmen

The Reachmen, also known as the Reachfolk, Witchmen of High Rock, and Witchfolk of the Reach, are a race of humans who are native to the Reach in southwestern Skyrim and the neighboring Western Reach in the east of High Rock, where some Reachfolk families originated from. They are believed to be distantly related to the Bretons. While Reachmen are distinct, they are also mixed with other races that make up Man, Mer, and allegedly even Daedra. Similarly to the Bretons, Aldmeri blood runs through their veins, which stems from the Direnni Hegemony's occupation of the Western Reach in the early First Era.

  • Aldmeri Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.
  • Cantrip. You know one cantrip of your choice from the Druid spell list. Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this cantrip. You choose which ability when you choose this lineage.
  • Rampage. When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a weapon attack on your turn, you can take a bonus action to move up to half your speed and make another weapon attack.
Redguard

Redguards are a human race that primarily inhabits the province of Hammerfell, but have roots that can be traced back to the continent of Yokuda. Known for their unique martial culture, they are reputed as brilliant battlefield tacticians who are quick to adopt new styles of armor and weaponry. They are the most naturally talented warriors in Tamriel. Hammerfell was formerly known as the Deathlands, as it hosts an arid climate with stifling, sandy winds, baking rocks that would brand one's feet, and terrible creatures that dwell in the crags and dunes. Regardless, conquering this hostile environment has become synonymous with Redguard culture. Aside from being physically blessed with hardy constitutions, they also have a resistance to poison and fleetness of foot. Redguards are known for their love of traveling, adventuring, and sailing the high seas: they can be seen commonly throughout Tamriel as sailors and mercenaries.

  • Poison Resistance. You have advantage on saving throws against poison and have resistance to poison damage.
  • Redguard Training. You have proficiency with sabers and scimitars.
  • Adrenaline Rush. You can take the Dash action as a bonus action. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. Additionally, whenever you use this trait, you gain a number of temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.
