Conjure Storm Atronach

7th-level Conjuration

Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (an amethyst worth at least 700 gp)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth a tempestuous daedra from the realms of oblivion. The daedra manifests in an unoccupied space you can see within range and uses the storm atronach stat block. The daedra disappears when it is reduced to 0 hit points or when the spell ends.

The daedra is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the daedra shares your initiative count and takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue the spirit any commands, it takes the Dodge action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the spell’s level appears in the storm atronach stat block.

Spell Lists. Sorcerer, Summoner, Warlock, Wizard

Storm Atronach
Large elemental, neutral
Armor Class: 11 + the level of the spell (natural armor)
Hit Points: 70 + 10 for each level of the spell above 7th
Speed: 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 13 (+1) 17 (+3) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 19 (+4)
Damage Immunities: poison, lightning, thunder
Condition Immunities: exhaustion, poisoned
Senses: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
Languages: Primordial, understands the languages you speak
Challenge: -
Cyclonic Aura: The area within 10 feet of the daedra is considered difficult terrain to creatures other than itself. In addition, ranged weapon and spell attack rolls targeting the daedra are made at disadvantage.
Magic Resistance: The daedra has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Actions
Multiattack. The daedra uses its Chain Lightning twice.
Chain Lightning. The daedra creates a bolt of lightning that strikes a target it can see within 90 feet of itself. The bolt then leaps from that target to another target within 30 feet of the first target. Each target must make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. A target takes 4d6 lightning damage on a failure or half as much on a success. This damage increases by 1d6 for each spell level beyond 7th.
Discharge. Melee Spell Attack: your spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d10 + 4 + the spell’s level lightning damage. The daedra may choose any number of spells of this spell's level or lower on the target to end. If it does so, the target takes bonus lightning damage equal to 1d10 per total level of the ended spells.
