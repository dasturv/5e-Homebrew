Conjure Frost Atronach

5th-level Conjuration

Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (a sapphire worth at least 500 gp)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth a frozen daedra from the realms of oblivion. The daedra manifests in an unoccupied space you can see within range and uses the frost atronach stat block. The daedra disappears when it is reduced to 0 hit points or when the spell ends.

The daedra is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the daedra shares your initiative count and takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue the spirit any commands, it takes the Dodge action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the spell’s level appears in the storm atronach stat block.

Spell Lists. Sorcerer, Summoner, Warlock, Wizard
