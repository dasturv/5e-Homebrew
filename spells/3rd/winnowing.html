Winnowing

3rd-level Necromancy

Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (15-foot sphere)
Components: V, S, M (a piece of dead grass)
Duration: Instantaneous

You call on the forces of death to strike down the weak. Each creature in a 15-foot sphere centered on you must make a Constitution saving throw. A creature takes 4d10 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature of CR 1/2 or lower has disadvantage on this saving throw.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 3rd. Additionally, the CR of creatures that make the save at disadvantage increases at certain levels: 5th (CR 1), 7th (CR 2) and 9th (CR 3)

Spell Lists. Sorcerer, Wizard
