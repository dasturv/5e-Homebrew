Bound Sword

2nd-level Conjuration

Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

You call the energies of oblivion to create a blade of solidified magic in your hand. This magic sword lasts until the spell ends. It counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. It deals 2d6 force damage on a hit and has the versatile property (versatile 2d8). Attacks rolls with the sword uses your spell attack bonus and damage rolls with it uses your spell casting ability instead of Strength or Dexterity. If you would land a critical hit with the sword, it deals only 1d6 extra force damage, or 1d8 when wielded with two hands.

If you drop the sword, it dissipates at the end of the turn. Thereafter, while the spell persists, you can use a bonus action to cause the sword to reappear in your hand.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a 3rd or 4th level spell slot, the damage increases to 3d6 (versatile 3d8). When you cast it using a 5th or 6th level spell slot, the damage increases to 4d6 (versatile 4d8). When you cast it using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases to 5d6 (versatile 5d8).

Spell Lists. Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
