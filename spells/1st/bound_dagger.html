Bound Dagger

1st-level Conjuration

Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

You call the energies of oblivion to create a dagger of solidified magic in your hand. This magic dagger lasts until the spell ends. It counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. It deals 2d4 force damage on a hit and has the light and thrown properties (range 20/60). Attacks rolls with the dagger uses your spell attack bonus and damage rolls with it uses your spell casting ability instead of Strength or Dexterity.

If you drop the dagger or throw it, it dissipates at the end of the turn. Thereafter, while the spell persists, you can use a bonus action to cause the dagger to reappear in your hand.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a 3rd level spell slot or higher, you can conjure two daggers at once, one in each hand. When you cast it using a 5th or 6th level spell slot, the damage for both daggers increases to 3d4. When you cast it using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage for both daggers increases to 4d4.

Spell Lists. Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
