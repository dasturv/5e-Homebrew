Soul Snare

1st-level Necromancy

Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S, M (an empty soul gem that can store the target's soul)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a creature you can see within range. You wrap the target in dark magics, attempting to capture its soul. If a non-humanoid creature of CR 1/2 or lower is reduced to 0 hit points while under the effects of this spell, it immediately dies and its soul fills the soul gem used in the casting of this spell. If a creature is under the effects of multiple soul trappings, the oldest effect takes priority.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using higher level spell slots, the maximum CR of creature you can soul trap increases. Up to CR 3 at 2nd level, CR 8 at 3rd level, CR 14 at 4th level. If you cast this spell using a 5th level or higher spell slot, you can soul trap any creature, including humanoids.

Spell Lists. Artificer, Warlock, Wizard
