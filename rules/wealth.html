Variant Rule: Wealth

Wealth in DnD can often either become tedious or simply ignored. This variant rule aims to remedy this by abstracting wealth to reduce bookkeeping while also allowing character wealth to shine in the story.

Wealth Level

A player's wealth level is an abstract measure of income, savings and capital. When using this variant rule, a player starts at Meager, though features can change this. A player stays at their current wealth level unless their situation would significantly change, such as losing their income for an extended period of time, having their property seized, or other such setback. Generally a wealthier player can weather larger and longer setbacks, whereas poorer ones may drop a level after even a week without income.

Investing and Devesting

Each wealth level has an investment cost, a one time cost required to upgrade to a new wealth level. You can only upgrade up one level at a time. When making an investment, you must pay its cost and spend an amount of downtime equal to the investment time. Once both are complete, you gain the benefits of your new wealth level.

If you wish to sell off your assets and move down a wealth level, you can devest. By spending downtime equal to half the investment time, you drop down one wealth level and gain gp equal to half the investment cost.

Lifestyle

Each wealth level has an associated lifestyle. You can sustain that lifestyle or any lower without needing to spend gp.

Spending Limits

Each wealth level has an amount of coin that, for all intents and purposes, is not worth tracking. Whenever a player makes a purchase below their spending limit, they do not need to subtract any coin from their inventory. There is no hard caps on how much a player can buy this way, but if it is abused, a good rule of thumb is poorer characters should only be able to make 1-2 purchases this way each day, whereas wealthier players can make 5-6 purchases this way.

Wealth Levels
Wealth Level Inv. Cost Inv. Time Spending Limit Lifestyle Description
Destitute - - - Wretched You have no assets nor income to speak of. All you own is what you carry with you.
Indigent 15 gp 1 week 1 cp Squalid You have a transient income and barely anything in the way of assets. You may pick up odd jobs or survive through begging or alms.
Meager 75 gp 2 weeks 5 cp Poor You have enough of an income to subsist, but not much beyond that. You may have some meager savings, a steady but poor paying job, or perhaps own a struggling business.
Stable 150 gp 1 month 2 sp Modest You have a consistent, if small, income alongside the ability to build savings. You may own a small business, work as an artisan or other skilled job, or even have a modest inheritence.
Well Off 450 gp 2 months 1 gp Comfortable You have a good stream of income and a good amount of capital. You may own a successful business, have a decently sized inheritence or live off of the generosity of a wealthy patron.
Wealthy 1,500 gp 4 months 5 gp Luxurious You have enough to mostly not need to worry about finances. You may earn revenue from multiple successful businesses, enjoy the wealth of minor nobility, or live off a massive inheritence.
Aristocratic 3,750 gp 8 months 15 gp Opulent You live in absolute abundance. You likely come from old money, enjoying nearly endless riches.
